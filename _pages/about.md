I train reinforcement learning agents to make decisions in orbit: which object a sensor should watch next, how to dock with a tumbling spacecraft, and how several spacecraft can maneuver together.

I'm an M.S. student in Astronautical Engineering at the Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), advised by Maj. Kullen Waggoner, Ph.D. My thesis applies deep reinforcement learning to **space situational awareness sensor tasking**, with a focus on catalogs where objects maneuver and a policy can quietly lose track of them. Alongside it, I've worked on **autonomous docking** with tumbling targets under coupled 6-DOF dynamics ([IAC 2026]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})) and **cooperative multi-spacecraft maneuver planning** with multi-agent RL ([IEEE NAECON 2026]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})).

I'm also an aerospace engineer and UAV pilot in the Turkish Air Force, with more than 1,000 flight hours in operational environments.
