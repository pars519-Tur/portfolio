About Me

M.S. Astronautical Engineering graduate student at the U.S. Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), focused on autonomous spacecraft operations, sensor network tasking, and space situational awareness (SSA).

My research applies deep reinforcement learning to space autonomy: tasking SSA sensor networks around maneuvering objects, autonomous docking with tumbling targets under coupled 6-DOF dynamics, and cooperative multi-spacecraft maneuver planning. This work has been presented at the International Astronautical Congress (IAC 2026) and IEEE NAECON 2026. I bring a unique perspective that merges hands-on operational aviation experience with advanced computational methods for space systems engineering.

Research

M.S. Thesis · Ongoing

Reinforcement Learning for SSA Sensor-Network Tasking

Deep-RL policies for tasking a ground-based Space Situational Awareness sensor network, extended to dynamic catalogs with maneuvering resident space objects.

  • Formulating sensor-to-target assignment as a sequential decision problem
  • Incorporating maneuvering spacecraft into training data and policy design
  • Implementing simulation and training infrastructure in Python and MATLAB
Reinforcement Learning SSA/SDA Sensor Networks Python MATLAB
Course Project

Robustness of RL Sensor Tasking to Unpredicted Maneuvers

Stress-testing a pretrained PPO sensor-tasking policy against maneuvers it never saw during training.

  • Maneuver-injection module that perturbs truth trajectories while leaving the estimator unaware
  • Zero-shot comparison of PPO against a greedy heuristic (3 sensors, ~100 objects) across maneuver rates
  • Tail-focused metrics: 90th-percentile covariance, unobserved maneuvering objects, reacquisition time
Robustness PPO Ray RLlib TensorFlow
IAC 2026 · Oral

RL-Based Autonomous Spacecraft Docking

Offset-port docking formulated as an MDP with fully coupled 6-DOF rotational–translational dynamics under J2 perturbations.

  • Captures attitude–position coupling neglected by decoupled Clohessy–Wiltshire formulations
  • Custom Gymnasium environment with docking-corridor constraints, thruster limits, and structured reward shaping; sweeps tracked in Weights & Biases
  • PPO agents dock with targets tumbling up to 2.5 deg/s, where a classical PD baseline fails beyond ~1 deg/s, at a quantified fuel cost (~3–3.6 vs. 1–2 m/s Δv)
PPO PyTorch Gymnasium 6-DOF Dynamics J2
IEEE NAECON 2026

Multi-Agent Cooperative Spacecraft Maneuver Planning

MAPPO framework with centralized training and decentralized execution for cooperative multi-spacecraft proximity operations.

  • Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire dynamics, benchmarked against an LQR + artificial-potential-field baseline
  • Behavior-cloning warm start; diagnosed and resolved a mid-training policy collapse, reaching 100% task success at the final checkpoint
  • Validated with Wilson score intervals, McNemar tests, and bootstrap confidence intervals
MAPPO Multi-Agent RL CTDE Behavior Cloning HCW
