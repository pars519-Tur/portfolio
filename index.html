Research

Reinforcement learning for SSA sensor-network tasking M.S. thesis, ongoing Deep-RL policies that decide which resident space object each ground sensor observes next, extended to catalogs where objects maneuver.
  • Sensor-to-target assignment formulated as a sequential decision problem.
  • Maneuvering spacecraft incorporated into training data and policy design.
  • Simulation and training infrastructure in Python and MATLAB.
Robustness of RL sensor tasking to unpredicted maneuvers Course project, CSCE 723 Stress-tests a pretrained PPO tasking policy against maneuvers it never saw in training, focusing on the objects it loses rather than the average.
  • Maneuver-injection module perturbs truth trajectories while the estimator stays unaware.
  • Zero-shot comparison of PPO against a greedy heuristic (3 sensors, ~100 objects) across maneuver rates.
  • Tail metrics: 90th-percentile covariance, maneuvering objects left unobserved, reacquisition time.
  • Ray RLlib, TensorFlow.
Autonomous docking with tumbling targets IAC 2026, oral presentation PPO agents dock with targets tumbling up to 2.5 deg/s, where a classical PD controller fails beyond about 1 deg/s.
  • Offset-port docking as an MDP with fully coupled 6-DOF rotational–translational dynamics under J2, capturing coupling that decoupled Clohessy–Wiltshire models ignore.
  • Custom Gymnasium environment with docking-corridor constraints, thruster limits, and structured reward shaping; sweeps tracked in Weights & Biases.
  • Fuel cost quantified: roughly 3–3.6 m/s Δv against 1–2 m/s for the baseline.
  • PyTorch, Gymnasium, Stable-Baselines3.
Cooperative multi-spacecraft maneuver planning IEEE NAECON 2026 MAPPO with centralized training and decentralized execution, benchmarked against an LQR plus artificial-potential-field controller.
  • Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire relative dynamics for proximity operations.
  • Behavior-cloning warm start; diagnosed and fixed a mid-training policy collapse, reaching 100% task success at the final checkpoint.
  • Results validated with Wilson score intervals, McNemar tests, and bootstrap confidence intervals.
